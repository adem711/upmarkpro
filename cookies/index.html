Cookies

Last updated: 17 October 2026 (draft)

In short

This site sets no cookies, neither its own nor third-party, and uses no analytics, advertising pixels or other tracking. That is why it shows no cookie banner.

What happens when you browse

  • No cookies and nothing stored in your browser (no cookies, localStorage or sessionStorage).
  • The site’s fonts and scripts are hosted on the site itself: no external service is contacted to load them.
  • No maps, videos or other content embedded from third parties.

Links to other services

The site links to external services such as WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram and the concept sites hosted on Vercel. When you open them you leave this site: those services have their own privacy and cookie policies.

If anything changes

If I later use analytics or other services that need cookies, I will update this page and ask for your consent before switching them on. Questions: hello@upmarkpro.com. See also the privacy notice.
